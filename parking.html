Parking geometry & ancillary requirements

Car parking bayMinimum 5.5 m × 2.7 m = 14.85 m² per standard car space.
Differently-abled parking3% of required car parking, subject to a minimum of 1 space. Locate near the entrance with maximum 30 m travel distance from the building entrance. Minimum parking-bay width: 3.6 m. This space is counted within the total required car-parking figure; it is not an additional space.
Parallel car parkingMinimum 6.0 m × 2.7 m.
Two-wheeler3 m² per space; minimum dimension 1.5 m. Two-wheeler parking area is 25% of mandatory car-parking area, with the number of spaces rounded up.
Driveway to parkingNormally minimum 3.0 m motorable width. For A1 with not more than 8 units, 2.4 m may be sufficient.
RampOne-way 3.5 m; two-way 5.5 m; curves 4.0 m / 6.0 m; slope not steeper than 1:7, subject to the occupancy/parking-count exception for two-way ramps.
Vehicular passageMinimum 4.0 m for rows of motor-car parking.
HeadroomMinimum 2.2 m for driveway, ramps and passages and for floors exclusively used for parking.

Rule basis used

Mixed occupancy note

If a building contains more than one occupancy, the applicable parking must be calculated occupancy-wise and the required areas earmarked accordingly. Fractions from separate occupancies are added and the total is then rounded up. This calculator currently calculates one selected occupancy at a time; a multi-occupancy worksheet can be added as the next version.
