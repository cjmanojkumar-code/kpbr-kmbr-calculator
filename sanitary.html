Current-rule calculator based on Rule 34 and Tables 13, 14, 15 & 15A, including the 2025 amendments.

1. Select Occupancy

Rule Notes

  • Group A1 residential occupancy is excluded from the Table 13/14/15 sanitation calculation.
  • Where a table says “and part thereof”, the calculator rounds upward.
  • The current amendment permits sanitation-facility area inside the building to be deducted from the floor area used for calculating sanitation requirements.
  • Table 15A: up to 2,000 m² = 100%; above 2,000 to below 5,000 = 90%; above 5,000 to below 8,000 = 80%; above 8,000 to below 12,000 = 70%; above 12,000 to below 18,000 = 60%; above 18,000 = 50%.
  • Information Technology / Technology Parks: sanitation requirement is 75% of office occupancy.
  • Specified visitor facilities include disabled-person WC and wash basin requirements.
